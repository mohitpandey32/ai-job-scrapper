# Phase 1: Product And Business Foundation

## Objective

Build an India-first AI-powered job aggregation and career assistant platform that helps candidates discover jobs, upload resumes, receive AI analysis, save jobs, and track applications.

## Approved Business Baseline

- Initial market: India
- Target users: job seekers across all roles
- Product model: subscription tool promoted and sold through Instagram
- Primary value proposition: collect job openings related to a user's field in one place
- MVP priority: faster launch
- AI requirement: resume upload and AI resume analysis included in MVP
- Aggregation requirement: continuous job aggregation for all users

## Problem Definition

Job seekers spend too much time searching across company sites, job boards, ATS boards, social posts, and public web pages. The experience is fragmented, duplicate-heavy, and poorly organized. Candidates also struggle to understand how well their resume matches available opportunities.

## User Personas

| Persona | Needs | Pain Points | MVP Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early-career candidate | Discover relevant openings, improve resume | Does not know which jobs fit | High |
| Experienced professional | Save time and track applications | Scattered job discovery and duplicate listings | High |
| Career switcher | Understand transferable skills | Hard to identify matching roles | Medium |
| Urgent job seeker | Fast discovery and organized pipeline | High stress and repetitive search | High |

## Functional Requirements

- Sign up and login
- Google authentication
- User profile and preferences
- Resume upload
- AI resume analysis
- Continuous job aggregation
- Job normalization
- Duplicate removal
- Search and filters
- Job details
- Saved jobs
- Application tracking with statuses: Saved, Applied, Interview, Assessment, Offer, Rejected

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Scalable to millions of users, jobs, searches, and AI workloads
- Secure handling of resume and profile data
- Observable ingestion and AI processing
- Fast search experience
- Reliable background workers
- Cost-aware AI usage
- Cloud deployment readiness

## MVP Scope

The MVP will include authentication, profiles, resume upload, AI resume analysis, continuous job ingestion from approved public sources, search, saved jobs, and application tracking.

## Out Of Scope

- Recruiter dashboard
- Mobile app
- Chrome extension
- WhatsApp and Telegram alerts
- Resume builder
- Salary prediction engine
- Fully autonomous application submission
- Fine-tuned ML models

## Success Metrics

| Category | Metric |
| --- | --- |
| Activation | Users uploading a resume |
| Engagement | Searches per user per week |
| Job quality | Duplicate rate |
| Workflow value | Saved jobs and tracked applications per user |
| AI value | Resume analysis helpfulness rating |
| Reliability | Ingestion success rate |
| Performance | Search p95 latency |
| Business | Subscription conversion rate |

## Roadmap

1. MVP: aggregation, resume analysis, search, saved jobs, application tracker
2. V1: job matching, cover letters, interview question generation
3. V2: skill gap analysis, learning recommendations, career coach
4. V3: alerts, Chrome extension, salary insights
5. V4: recruiter tools and analytics
